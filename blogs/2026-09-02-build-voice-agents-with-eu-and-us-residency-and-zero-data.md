Two questions come up in almost every enterprise evaluation: where exactly does the inference run, and what do you keep? Data residency pins your Text-to-Speech, Speech-to-Text and Speech-to-Speech sessions to our EU servers, or to our US servers, enforced at the server and reported on every response, so you can assert on it instead of trusting a hostname. Zero Data Retention decides whether the text and audio processed by our servers survive the call at all.
