Most voice agents speak with one preselected voice, whoever is on the line. Once a voice can be created from a text description, the voice becomes another property your code computes from context. This article builds the full pipeline: classify the message, map the signals to a Voice Design prompt, resolve it to an approved voice on the hot path, and design the missing ones in the background.
