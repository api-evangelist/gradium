Developers building voice agents ask us for voices matched to the use case in front of them: a Québécoise receptionist for a Montréal dealership, a Paulista support agent for a São Paulo fintech, a narrator in his sixties with the authority of a lecture hall. Briefs outnumber any catalog. Voice Design starts from a prompt: write one or two sentences, get complete new voices back in seconds, keep the one you want.
